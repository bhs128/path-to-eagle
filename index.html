How to read

Every requirement from joining to Eagle, laid out like a research tree and built from the Scout's own TroopWebHost records. Each board of review is a gateway between two rank zones; its requirements sit in the zone before it, where the work happens. Finished ranks stay on the map in green.

Bar fill = progress; ends where it's neededGateway: board of review Timer: can't be rushed
Three levels. Compact shows just names and progress. Focus hides finished requirements and groups the rest by topic. All shows every rank requirement and every open merit badge requirement.
Bars are flexible. A bar starts today and ends at the gateway that needs it; its fill is the progress so far. A diamond marks a troop session or a start-by date.
Service counts per rank. Star needs 6 hours done while First Class, and Life needs 6 more while Star. Hours from before don't carry over.

Drag to move. Scroll or pinch to zoom; two-finger swipe pans. Click any node, row, or bar for details; press Esc, click ✕, or click empty map to clear.
