# Path to Eagle

A tech-tree map of a Scout's path from joining to Eagle, built from the reports a parent or Scout can download from TroopWebHost.

**[Open the page →](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/path-to-eagle/)**

## What it does

- Loads three TroopWebHost exports through a step-by-step wizard: Scouting History (required), Uncompleted Merit Badge Requirements, and the troop's Upcoming Events Summary.
- Draws every rank, requirement, merit badge, service hour and leadership position on a pannable map, with the lines showing what depends on what.
- Builds a ranked **Do Next** list and estimates each board of review from the troop calendar (advancement nights, campouts, courts of honor), tenure and timed requirements.
- Shows full requirement text for all merit badges and checks off the finished ones.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The files you load are read on your device and never uploaded; addresses, phone numbers and emails in the exports are ignored. If you tick **Remember on this device**, the Scout's records are kept in this browser's local storage only, and **Forget this Scout** clears them.

## Where to get the files (TroopWebHost)

1. **Scouting History:** ☰ menu → My Stuff → My Scouts → your Scout's row → Rank Status → Scouting History Report → Open in Excel.
2. **Merit badge requirements:** ☰ menu → My Stuff → My Scouts → your Scout's row → Merit Badges → Uncompleted Merit Badge Requirements → Open in Excel.
3. **Troop calendar:** ☰ menu → Calendar → Event Reports → Upcoming Events Summary → Open in Excel.
